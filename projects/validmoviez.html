ValidMoviez helps users discover movie suggestions through a recommendation API. The mobile app brings user accounts and personalized picks together in one flow.

What I contributed

  • Developed responsive login and home screens with JavaScript and React Native.
  • Implemented Firebase Authentication and Realtime Database services for user management and storage.
  • Integrated the recommendation API, including data fetching and error handling.
  • Collaborated with two teammates on wireframes and the app's technical plan.
