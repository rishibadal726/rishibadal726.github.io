PROJECT OVERVIEW

Learning to land.

The agent learns to steer a lunar module toward a safe landing between two flags. Our team used repeated training runs and reward tracking to assess its progress.

What I contributed

  • Led training and evaluation across 2,000 episodes.
  • Logged rewards and saved model checkpoints every 250 episodes to compare progress.
  • Used Matplotlib plots to inspect trends and identify crash penalties.
  • Adjusted target-network synchronization to stabilize learning after early negative scores.
