PROJECT STORY

From prototype
to presentation.

The prototype gathered environmental sensor data, and the interface displayed its readings so the team could monitor the subsystem and present its behavior through NASA HUNCH competition rounds.

What I contributed

  • Built React and JavaScript UI components with HTML and CSS.
  • Designed and refined layouts based on feedback, improving usability and consistency.
  • Collaborated with three teammates through multiple competition rounds and the final presentation.
